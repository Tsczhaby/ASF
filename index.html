Enlist in the Wardrobe Watch

Manifesto

Style is a civic duty. Our inspectors keep the streets pristine, monochrome, and meticulously tailored. No clashing palettes. No ill-fitting garments. No compromise.

Precision

Every outfit is evaluated against the ASF Grid — proportion, texture, alignment. Deviations are recorded, corrected, and archived.

Uniformity

Wardrobe quotas ensure consistent tones. Grayscale gradients are assigned monthly, creating synchronized cityscapes.

Compliance

Mobile patrols provide on-the-spot adjustments, issuing replacement garments and immediate styling directives.

Protocols & Penalties

  • Pattern Moratorium: Stripes and checks require quarterly permits. Unauthorized prints are confiscated.
  • Texture Curfew: Velvet and leather are nighttime materials. Daylight violations trigger mandatory restyling.
  • Accessory Index: Bracelets, collars, and cuffs must align with the district’s shine allowance.
  • Footwear Formation: Laces are symmetrical, boots are polished, heels are measured to the millimeter.
